Today is the day of the FYP competition. The day I look forward to join since the start of my final year. It's my only chance to make a difference, to put out to the world a project that I am truly passionate about. I tirelessly embarked over the weekend to get my poster printed and prepare the media for the booth setup. I knew I had to make an impression in the crowd. At the heat of the moment, I fell short on the judging for the finalist spot. The judge exposed a flaw I haven't considered and quick to label my ineptitude as a developer, for one oversight. It was an abrupt and piercing stab, and hurts even more the longer I thought about it. I did not make it to the finals. I felt as if all my hard work was invalidated in an instant. Whispers telling me that there were some bias or unfairness going on during the shortlisting, but I could not confirm that. Regardless, I stood by my booth with the project I've worked on for 4 months till the very end, as I watched the finalists pitch on stage, with the judgement replaying in my head to no end.

Freedom, to achieve my dreams is something I am constantly chasing. As I run faster, the illusion begin to form in my mind that I feel like I am getting closer. The longer I stay in the illusion, the more ecstatic I become, like I could exit the loop and finally grasp that unknowable thing that I was searching all this time. And for the second time in my life, the illusion shatters in front of me again. I knew this feeling too well. The same deep dark emptiness in my heart that makes me cower in fear and trip into survival mode. My first time was when I was 21 at the dusk of my chapter in Germany, and now here as I am about to graduate my degree. Turns out, in pursuit of standing out, I was running in circles and never went anywhere. Hard work is never rewarded treated equally because life is never fair to begin with. I knew I wouldn't have any other chance after this as I begin my true adulthood. In the end, I will successfully graduate to become another cog in the machine of a grander design. To become fodder to the beast. What a life this is.

All that's left is a vengeance. I refuse to drown in the mistakes of my past again. I will never settle for mediocrity. I do not want to live constantly succumbing to expectations. I will try again.
